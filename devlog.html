Devlog

Launch

Sky Runner goes live

First public version of Sky Runner shipped — a colorful endless runner playable straight in the browser, no install needed. Built as a self-contained HTML5 canvas game with a difficulty ramp, local high-score saving, and a rewarded-ad system for revives and extra lives.

Ongoing

AdSense & monetization setup

Working through Google AdSense approval to support the project with lightweight, optional advertising — including rewarded video ads via Google's Ad Placement API, so players can choose to watch a short ad for extra lives rather than having ads forced on them.

Planned

What's next

Future updates may include new obstacle types, a day/night visual cycle, and additional small browser games added to this site over time.
